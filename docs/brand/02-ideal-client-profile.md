# Ideal Client Profile

Aligned to live site copy (www.foodsense.tech). Brand OS follows the live framing.

## Who We Serve Best

**Industry:** Restaurant and foodservice — QSR, fast-casual, and franchise-based multi-unit concepts. Not fine dining. **Not a customer: single-unit independents.**

**Company size (who we write to):** **10+ units.** Large enough to need stack standardization and program leadership; often without a dedicated internal digital / technology lead.

**Emerging groups:** Groups under 10 units are not the primary reader. A 5–7 year growth pipeline can still start a conversation (scoped engagement), but the homepage and ICP center on 10+ unit operators.

**Stage:** Growth or professionalization — expanding stores / markets, or replacing starter systems with scalable platforms. Ready to invest; outgrown spreadsheet ops.

**Geography:** Latin America, the Caribbean, and the United States. Multi-market complexity is normal (regulatory, vendor, franchise maturity).

**Primary readers:** VP of IT / Tech, VP of Digital Channels, CDTO, CEO, Country GM. Franchise owners, COO, CFO, and CMO should still be able to read the page in one pass.

## Pain Points That Signal Fit

- "We have 6 vendors and none of them talk to each other"
- "We're growing but our tech stack is the same as when we had 5 stores"
- "Global gave us a roadmap but no one to implement it locally"
- "We tried a kiosk / loyalty / delivery rollout and it stalled"
- "We don't know what data we have or where it lives"
- "Our POS is end-of-life and we don't know how to migrate without shutting down"

## Qualifying Criteria

**Hell Yes:**
- Multi-unit operator (**10+ locations**) needing stack standardization
- No internal technology / digital lead, or one who is overwhelmed
- Budget for technology work this year
- Executive sponsor who treats tech as an operational lever
- Willing to work phased (Advisory → Fractional or Project)
- Operating in LATAM, Caribbean, or US markets

**Strong Maybe (conversation, not homepage):**
- 10–20 units scaling fast with a clear 50+ plan
- Has an internal team but needs a layer above execution
- Wants a vendor assessment or second opinion before a platform bet

**Not Right Now:**
- Single-unit independents
- Under 10 units with no growth plan
- One-time strategy deck with no implementation path
- No budget and no executive buy-in
- Looking for a software vendor, not a consultancy
- Needs brand / marketing strategy (not our lane)

## Red flags (sales only — never on the website)

- “Just build us an app” — they want a dev shop.
- “Do this free as a pilot; we’ll pay later.”
- Last consultant’s deck sat unused and nothing has changed.
- “All locations in two weeks.” They will not accept phased rollout.
- The GM / franchisee does not know this is happening (no sponsor).
- “Evaluate 15 vendors first.” Analysis paralysis.
- Decision by committee with no named owner.
- Scope shifting before the SOW is signed.
- Single-unit operator who “thinks big.” Thinking big is not being big.

**Brand OS PDF note:** The source PDF still lists Hell Yes as 20–500+ units. Website 2.0 and this file stay on **10+ units**. Do not drift the homepage back to 20–500.

## Decision Makers

- **CEO / MD (Franchisee):** ROI, growth, not getting burned by a bad tech bet
- **VP / Director of Operations:** Execution consistency, minimal disruption
- **IT / Technology Lead:** Integration architecture, no new technical debt
- **CFO:** Budget predictability, measurable outcomes
- **Country GM / Franchise Development:** Scalable tech for market entry
