# Ideal Client Profile

## Who We Serve Best

**Industry:** Restaurant and foodservice, specifically quick-service (QSR), fast-casual, and franchise-based concepts. We don't serve fine dining, independent single-units, or non-food retail.

**Company Size:** 20–500+ units. Large enough to need real technology infrastructure, but often without a dedicated internal digital/technology team to build and manage it.

**Stage:** Growth-mode. They're either actively expanding (opening new stores, entering new markets) or professionalizing (replacing starter systems with scalable platforms). They've outgrown spreadsheets and are ready to invest.

**Geography:** Latin America, Caribbean, and US markets. We're built for multi-market complexity: different regulatory environments, different vendor availability, different franchise maturity levels.

## Pain Points That Signal Fit

- "We have 6 vendors and none of them talk to each other"
- "We're growing but our tech stack is the same as when we had 5 stores"
- "Global gave us a roadmap but no one to implement it locally"
- "We tried a kiosk/loyalty/delivery rollout and it stalled"
- "We don't know what data we have or where it lives"
- "Our POS is end-of-life and we don't know how to migrate without shutting down"

## Qualifying Criteria

**Hell Yes:**
- Multi-unit operator (20+ locations) with active growth plans
- No internal technology/digital lead, or one who's overwhelmed
- Budget allocated for technology investment this year
- Executive sponsor who understands tech is an operational lever
- Willing to commit to a phased approach
- Operating in LATAM, Caribbean, or US markets

**Not Right Now:**
- Under 10 units with no clear growth plan
- Wants a one-time strategy deck with no implementation
- No budget and no executive buy-in
- Looking for a software vendor, not a consultancy

## Decision Makers

- **CEO/MD (Franchisee):** Cares about ROI, growth, not getting burned by a bad tech bet
- **VP/Director of Operations:** Cares about execution consistency, minimal disruption
- **IT/Technology Lead:** Cares about integration architecture, no technical debt
- **CFO:** Cares about budget predictability, measurable outcomes
- **Franchise Development:** Cares about scalable tech for new market entry
