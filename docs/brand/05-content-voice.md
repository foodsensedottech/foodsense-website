# Content Voice

## Voice Attributes

- **Direct.** Say what you mean in the fewest words possible. No hedging, no "we believe," no filler.
- **Pragmatic.** Everything we write solves a problem or moves something forward.
- **In Control.** Calm, authoritative, unflinching. Never reactive, never defensive.
- **Simple.** Complex technology explained in operator language. No jargon for jargon's sake.
- **Process-Driven.** Sequential, logical, structured. Numbered steps over flowing paragraphs.

## Tone by Context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Proposals | Confident, structured, data-first | "Phase 1 targets 3 stores over 14 days. Success metric: zero ticket errors at close." |
| Client emails | Professional, concise, action-oriented | "Attached is the updated timeline. Two changes from last week." |
| Website copy | Opinionated, operator-first, conversational | "Every franchise has the same problem: 6 vendors, zero integration." |
| LinkedIn | Opinionated, story-driven, real examples | Share wins, frameworks, and operator POV. Never salesy. |

## Writing Principles

1. Lead with the action. Context comes second.
2. One idea per sentence. Short sentences.
3. Step-by-step over narrative. Think Lego instructions.
4. Data is a sentence, not a paragraph.
5. Cut the warm-up. Start with substance.
6. Use structure aggressively (headers, bullets, tables).
7. Write for the person doing the work.

## Vocabulary

**Use:** rollout, deployment, integration, adoption, maturity, blocker, phased, reconciliation, workstream, KPI

**Avoid:** synergy, leverage (verb), ideate, best-in-class, disruptive, innovative, solutions (standalone), stakeholder (client-facing), learnings

**Our phrases:** "Signal vs. noise", "Operator-first", "Test, prove, scale"
